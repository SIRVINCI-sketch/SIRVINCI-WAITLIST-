INSTRUCTOR & ORIGIN

Why I'm Building This

Sirvinci Creative Studio started as a place to help businesses and individuals bring their ideas to life through design and digital work. But somewhere along the way, people started reaching out for something different — not a project, but guidance. “Can you teach me this?” “Is there a course?”

I realized the real gap wasn't interest — it was access. Most design education assumes you already own a laptop and speak the language of design. Most people messaging me had neither. What they did have was a smartphone and Canva already sitting on it, completely underused.

That's the gap Sirvinci Academy is built to close — for total beginners and for people who already design but never learned the “why” behind their own work. Design isn't a talent you're born with. It's a skill, taught step by step, with no one left behind.

This is the course I wish existed when I was starting out. Let's build it together.

Sirvinci (David Ogebe)
Founder, Sirvinci Creative Studio
